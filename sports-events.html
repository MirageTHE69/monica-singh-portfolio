Why it matters

A sports stage runs on live energy, not a fixed clock.

Player auctions swing on bids nobody scripted, matches run long or short, and a trophy presentation has to land the moment the game ends, not five minutes later. The host has to move with the game, not against it.

Monica has hosted cricket league auctions, corporate sports days and tournament finals across Ahmedabad and Gujarat, keeping commentary sharp and the crowd engaged in Hindi or English as the moment needs.

Frequently asked

How early should we book a date? +

League season (October–March) fills up 4 to 6 weeks in advance. For a standalone auction or corporate sports day, 2 to 3 weeks is usually sufficient.

Do you host player auctions as well as match days? +

Yes. Monica hosts the full arc — auction night, opening ceremony, live match commentary and the closing trophy presentation.

What languages do you host in? +

Hindi and English. Monica switches naturally between them based on what the crowd and organizing committee are most comfortable with.

Do you travel outside Ahmedabad? +

Yes, Monica regularly hosts league matches and corporate sports days across Vadodara, Surat, Rajkot and other cities in Gujarat.
