About Monica

Ten years of reading a room and never losing it.

Based in Ahmedabad, Monica hosts sangeets, haldi ceremonies, product launches and award functions across Gujarat. She moves easily between Hindi, English and Gujarati, and between a dance-floor sangeet and a corporate stage.

Every event gets a written run-of-show, rehearsed transitions, and a host who keeps the energy where it needs to be — up for a baraat, composed for a keynote.
