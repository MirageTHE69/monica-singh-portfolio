Why it matters

A sangeet runs on timing more than anything else.

Ten to fifteen family performances, a live band or DJ, surprise entries, and a room full of relatives who all want a turn on the mic. Without someone actively running the evening, it either drags or turns into chaos.

Monica has hosted sangeets across Ahmedabad and greater Gujarat for over a decade, working from a run-of-show built with the family in advance, in Hindi, English or Gujarati as the crowd needs.
