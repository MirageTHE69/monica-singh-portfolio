Why it matters

A corporate stage runs on precision more than energy.

Leadership speeches, sponsor mentions, video rolls and award reveals all have to land on cue in front of a room that includes clients, press and the board. There's no space for dead air or a mistimed handover.

Monica has hosted product launches, annual meets and award nights for brands across Ahmedabad and Gujarat for over a decade, working from a run-of-show built with the organizing team in advance, in Hindi or English as the audience needs.

Frequently asked

How early should we book a date? +

Peak season for award nights and annual meets (October–March) fills up 2 to 3 months in advance. For other months, 3 to 4 weeks is usually sufficient.

Do you work with our internal events or PR team? +

Yes. Monica reviews the agenda, speaker list and brand guidelines with your team ahead of time so every cue and mention lands correctly.

What languages do you host in? +

Hindi and English. Monica switches naturally between them based on what the audience and leadership are most comfortable with.

Do you travel outside Ahmedabad? +

Yes, Monica regularly hosts corporate events across Vadodara, Surat, Rajkot and other cities in Gujarat, as well as multi-city launches elsewhere in India.
